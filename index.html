Emersion

In today's working life place and time become more and more negligible for a whole generation of creative professionals, IT-consultants and "computer aided designers". The role of the physical work space is changing: there is no need to store massive amounts of pencils, paper, or rulers. In the future even the desktop itself may loose its right to exist. New virtual, mobile and trancending products will replace its central role.

The role of designer will be to connect virtual and real life in such a cyber-physical working practice and at times to pull the worker back into a reality reclaiming time and space.
